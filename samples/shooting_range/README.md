# Shooting range (F6.08 exercise)

A raycast shooting range that uses the 3D physics of API 6: layers, `LayerMask`, raycasts, a trigger zone and Rigidbody knock-back.
Everything here is generated by `tools/make_sample.py` (cubes, spheres and flat colours; nothing is ripped). `Assets/Scripts/Range.cpp`
is the one hand-written file.

| Layer | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| 8 `Target` | five red slabs (Rigidbody, mass 1.5) the shots must hit |
| 9 `Ignore` | two blue decoy panels standing in front of targets 1 and 3 |
| 10 `Zone` | the trigger box in front of the shooter (`ZoneCounter` counts entries) |
| 11 `Runner` | a yellow ball that is pushed back and forth through the zone |

* A shot is `Physics::Raycast(muzzle, dir, &hit, 80, mask)` with a mask that leaves out `Ignore` and `Runner`, so a decoy never blocks the line of sight.
  A second, unmasked ray reports what the shot went through: `ignored=Decoy0`.
* A hit target gets `AddForce(dir * 6, ForceMode::Impulse)` and is destroyed 0.7 s later. A miss is logged as `miss`.
* `Physics.json` lists the pair `Ignore`/`Target` by layer name (resolved through `TagManager.json`): bodies of those layers pass through each other.

Scenes: `Range` (the nub aims, South fires) and `RangeBot` (the bot plays: 14 shots, every 4th aimed 2 m off its target; this is the AUTOSTART scene of the PSP run).

```bash
python3 samples/shooting_range/tools/make_sample.py                  # regenerate the project (Range.cpp is kept)
tools/ci/physics_range.sh [linux] [psp] [desktop]                    # the CI job: headless log, PSP ELF in PPSSPPHeadless with frame dumps, GL screenshots
```

Log lines (`rensga: range ...`): `ready`, `shot=<n> hit=<name> dist=<cm> ignored=<decoy|none>`, `shot=<n> miss`, `zone enter=<n> by=Runner`,
`done shots=14 hits=<n> blocked=<n> zone=<n>`. The Linux and PSP runs print the same `done` line (the simulation is deterministic).
